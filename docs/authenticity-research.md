## Safe implementation recommendations pending area-specific verification

These are fictional design choices inspired by ordinary neighbourhood life. They are not claims about the exact soundscape, streets, architecture, or residents of Hanover Park.

- Keep the current map fictional. Do not rename generic blocks, shops, rank routes, or pitch after real places unless their existence/location is verified.
- Build a calm everyday soundscape: spatial wind, pavement/dirt footsteps, vehicle engine and road wash, occasional horns, a ball bouncing near the pitch, distant unintelligible crowd chatter, and an original low-volume shop-radio motif. No real recordings, imitated residents, copyrighted radio, or default gunshots/sirens.
- Make sounds responsive: footsteps only while moving, pitch activity only near the pitch, idle/rev engine while driving, simple panning and distance attenuation. Start audio after a user gesture; provide visible mute and volume controls and persist their state.
- Improve depth/readability with coherent building fronts and roof planes, warm sunlight and ground shadows, window recesses/rails, washing lines, painted shopfronts, curb corners, repairs and surface variation. Visual detail should tell stories of community use and care as well as age.
- Do not treat the entire Cape Flats as one accent, religion, or personality. Use ordinary English and occasional familiar Afrikaans phrases; do not stack slang into every line or claim the lines are resident-approved. Realistic voice performances would benefit from local speakers and consented reference recordings.
- Keep help text separate from character speech where possible. Give each fictional person a purpose and warm natural phrasing.
